Build a real-time, realistic-but-painterly scene of the Mataf around the Kaaba in Masjid al-Haram, Makkah,
seen at human scale. It only needs to look beautiful and let me walk (and, with a key, fly) through it.

PRIORITIES (in this order when they conflict)
1. Visual quality
2. Stable frame pacing at the target spec
3. Small bundle size

CONSTRAINTS
- No Three.js/Babylon.js or other engines. WebGPU rendered.
- Everything procedural: no texture/model/audio files. Deliverable is a single self-contained index.html.
  Report minified size.
- Zero per-frame JS allocations (no GC stutter). Preallocate all buffers.
- Sacred site: no human figures at all and no depiction of any person; the spaces are empty. No interiors:
  never inside the Kaaba; its door stays closed. No synthesized adhan, talbiyah or Qur'an recitation; no
  scripture or any other text rendered anywhere. The Kiswah band is a gold embroidered pattern that reads as
  calligraphy from a distance but contains no legible words or letters; the clock tower carries no lettering.
  Finials are stacked spheres with an upturned crescent, checked in silhouette. Buildings are respectful
  approximations, not replicas; the geography is composed.

TARGET
- Omarchy Linux, Chrome 157, RTX 3070, 60 Hz+ monitor
- 2560x1440 at full resolution. GPU frame time <= 11 ms average, p99 <= 16 ms during a scripted 60 s
  fly-through.

SCENE
- The Kaaba in its black Kiswah with the gold embroidered band two-thirds up, the gold door raised above the
  floor on the north-east wall, the Black Stone corner in its silver frame, the low curved wall of the Hijr
  Ismail opposite the north-west wall, the gold water spout above it
- Maqam Ibrahim in its gold-and-glass enclosure in front of the door
- The Mataf: white marble that stays cool, polished, faintly mirroring
- The Ottoman arcades around it: pointed arches on slender columns, each bay under a small dome with a
  crescent finial; the later multi-storey arcades behind; many tall minarets
- The Abraj al-Bait clock tower to the south, towering over everything, with plain clock faces (no lettering)
- The bare granite mountains of the Makkah valley and the city on their slopes, fading into haze
- Life: pigeons circling, dust in the low sun
- Time menu: Dawn (default), Golden hour, Night (lit arcades, lamps, minaret lights, lit clock face, stars,
  moon, readable shadows)

CONTROLS
WASD walk, mouse look (pointer lock, or drag), Shift to run, F toggles flight (E lifts off, Q lands)

VERIFICATION
- Visual critic subagent that sees ONLY the renders: 6 fixed poses at 2560x1440 plus flight frames.
  Iterate until nothing above "minor" or 5 critic iterations.
- GPU timestamp queries plus a frame-time log from the scripted fly-through: average, p99, max.

DONE WHEN: both gates pass (or the iteration cap is reached). Deliver index.html, bundle size, perf report and
the critic's final review.
